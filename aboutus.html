About CampusFind

CampusFind is a smart Lost & Found platform developed to help students, teachers and university staff quickly report, search and recover lost items across the campus.

Our Mission

Our mission is to create a secure and reliable platform where anyone can report lost or found items and reconnect them with their rightful owners quickly and easily.

Our Vision

We believe every university should have a modern digital Lost & Found system that saves time, improves communication and reduces the stress of losing valuable belongings.

Our Features

  • Lost Item Posting
  • Found Item Posting
  • Search Lost & Found Items
  • Profile Dashboard
  • Messaging System
  • University Map
  • Secure Login & Registration

Why CampusFind?

CampusFind provides an easy-to-use interface where users can post, search and communicate securely. It helps reduce the time required to recover lost belongings while making campus life more convenient.
